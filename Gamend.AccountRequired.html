    Gamend.AccountRequired (gamend_core v1.1.1360)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    What a guest may not do until it registers. A guest is an account with no email and no sign-in provider, only a device or a browser (Gamend.Accounts.User.anonymous?/1). The host picks the features:

    config :gamend_core, Gamend.AccountRequired, [:quest_claims, :leaderboards]

    Default: none, so a guest does everything a registered player does.

    • :quest_claims — Gamend.Quests.claim/3 answers {:error, :account_required} (the API's 403 account_required), and an auto_claim quest does not pay itself. Progress still counts: a completed quest waits, and pays once the account is registered.
    • :leaderboards — a guest's records are kept but never listed on a public board (one not hidden): no list, count or rank includes them, so a guest moves nobody. Their own record still reads, ranked where it would stand, with hidden: true (Gamend.Leaderboards.get_user_record/3, the API's GET …/me). Registering lists them at once: each record keeps a guest flag that the database sets from the account and moves when the account gains its first identity (or loses its last).

    Both apply in core, so every client meets the same rule; a client reads the 403 or the hidden flag to say why and offer registration.
