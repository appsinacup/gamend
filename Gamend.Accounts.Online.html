    Gamend.Accounts.Online (gamend_core v1.1.1428)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    A player online, one way for every client. The SDK's game socket (GamendWeb.UserChannel) and a host's own page (a LiveView the player plays in) call the same functions, so users.is_online, friends' online dots, lobby, party and group presence, matchmaking's offline prune and the abandoned-lobby reaper see them alike.

    • connected/3: the socket's process tracked on the user's presence topic (Gamend.Presence.users_topic/1) with its kind ("socket" or "page"), users.is_online set, the user's lobby, party and groups told, and the game's after_socket_connected/3 called.
    • heartbeat/1: last_seen_at refreshed. A socket calls it every heartbeat_ms/0, so StalePresenceSweeper leaves a live player alone.
    • disconnected/2: when no other socket of theirs is left, the user's matchmaking tickets cancelled (an accept they were asked for declined) and users.is_online cleared. Checked again a moment later for a socket another node had not replicated yet.

    watch/2 does all three (as a "page") for a process with no terminate/2 to rely on: a LiveView's is not called when the process is killed or crashes. It monitors the process, keeps its heartbeat and runs disconnected/2 when it goes. A node going down takes its watchers with it, and StalePresenceSweeper marks those players offline.

    config :gamend_core, Gamend.Accounts.Online, watch: false

    leaves watch/2 at connected/2, with no watcher (tests: a watcher outlives the test's database sandbox, and its writes fail once the test is done).

    Summary

    Functions

    connected(pid, user_id, kind \\ "socket")

    pid, a socket of user_id's, is open: tracked on the user's presence topic (its meta kind: "socket", a client's UserChannel, or "page", a host's page, watch/2), users.is_online set, the user's lobby, party and groups told, and the game's after_socket_connected/3 called with it, every socket (after_user_online/1 is once a session). Answers the user as written, or {:error, reason} when the account is gone.

    disconnected(user_id, pid)

    pid, a socket of user_id's, closed (or is closing). With no other socket of theirs left, their matchmaking tickets are cancelled, users.is_online is cleared and their lobby, party and groups told, then checked again after a moment and the flag put back if a socket is there after all.

    heartbeat(user_id)

    A socket of user_id's is still open: last_seen_at refreshed.

    heartbeat_ms()

    How often an open socket calls heartbeat/1.

    watch(pid, user_id)

    pid (a LiveView, any process with no terminate/2 to rely on) is a socket of user_id's for as long as it lives: connected/2 now, a heartbeat/1 every heartbeat_ms/0, disconnected/2 when it ends however it ends. One watcher process per socket, under its own supervisor.
