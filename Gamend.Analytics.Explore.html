    Gamend.Analytics.Explore (gamend_core v1.1.1361)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Reading the event rows (analytics_events) one question at a time: one event, a group of them or all, over a range of UTC days, in one segment (Gamend.Analytics.Segment), narrowed by any of the events' dimensions (lang = ro, mode = daily) or to one account. The admin Analytics pages' Explore and People tabs are built on it.

    A filter is a map (filter/1 fills the defaults):

    %{names: ["web.game.finished"] | :all, from: ~D[…], to: ~D[…],
  segment: :all, where: %{"lang" => "ro"}, user_id: nil}

    A where value of "none" matches the events without that field, the way a breakdown names them.

    Rows are an account's only (a visitor's event is a counter), so every number here is about accounts. counter_series/4 reads the counters, where everyone is, for the same event.

    The daily counter behind an event, everyone included, for the filter's days: the bare name with no where, name.dim:value with one, nil with more (no counter holds two dimensions at once) or with several names.

    A filter from options: names: (default :all), to: (default today), from: or days: (default 30, ending on to), segment:, where:, user_id:.

    What one account has done: %{first_day, last_day, days_active, days_active_30, events, by_name} — its activity days (all of them, and in the last 30), its event rows, and those by name ([%{name, events, last_at}], most first).

    rows(filter, opts \\ [])

    One page of the filter's rows, newest first, each with its account (user, nil once the account is deleted): %{rows, total}. Options: page: (from 1), page_size: (default 50).

    series(filter)

    Every day of the filter's range: [%{day, events, people}], oldest first.

    totals(filter)

    The filter's events and distinct people.
