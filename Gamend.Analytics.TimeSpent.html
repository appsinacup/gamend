    Gamend.Analytics.TimeSpent (gamend_core v1.1.1519)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    How long accounts spend in the game, worked out from what is already stored: nothing is collected for it.

    Every row an account leaves is a moment it was there: its analytics events (analytics_events, an account's only), the start of each play (results.started_at) and each thing that happened in one (result_events, started_at + elapsed_ms). Sorted per account, the time from one moment to the next is time spent when it is at most idle_seconds (default 5 minutes); a longer gap is the account away, and counts nothing. A declared span (spans: a test started and finished, with nothing stored in between) counts up to span_seconds (default 2 hours) from its start to its end.

    Each gap is filed under where the account was at its start: the AREA of the earlier moment, and its DETAIL where there is one:

    • a play's moment: the area results (default "plays"), the detail the play's kind (a game);
    • an event named in dim's list: the value of that field ("section" of a page view), passed through areas;
    • any other event: its catalogue group (Gamend.Analytics.Catalog), passed through areas.

    So the numbers are a floor: the time after an account's last moment of a visit (reading the last page, the end of a round) is not counted, and a visitor with no account leaves no rows, so is in none of it. Days are UTC days, as DAU is. The host says how its rows read in its catalogue's overview/0, under time (Catalog.overview/0).

    report(days, today \\ Date.utc_today(), opts \\ [])

    @spec report(pos_integer(), Date.t(), keyword()) :: map()

    The time spent over the last days UTC days ending on today (today partial), for the admin Analytics Time tab:

    • seconds, people (accounts with any time), account_days, per_day_avg / per_day_median (seconds per account on a day it came), per_person (seconds per account over the window);
    • series: per day %{day, people, seconds, avg, median};
    • buckets: account-days by how long, %{label, count};
    • areas: %{id, label, people, account_days, seconds, avg, share}, avg per account-day that used the area, most time first;
    • details: the same per {area, detail} where there is a detail.

    Options: segment:.
