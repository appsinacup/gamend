    Gamend.Bans.Ban (gamend_core v1.1.1367)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    One ban: an IP address, an account or a chat sender kept out until expires_at (nil is permanent), or until someone lifts it.

    A row is kept after it ends, lifted or expired, so a target's history reads as its rows; retention deletes ended rows after bans_days. A ban is active while lifted_at is nil and expires_at is nil or still ahead.

    • kind — :ip (subject is the address, an IPv6 one as its /64), :account (subject is the user id, also in user_id) or :chat, a mute (subject and user_id are the muted player).
    • scope / scope_ref_id — a chat ban's reach: "global" (every chat, friend DMs included; no room id) or "lobby", "group" or "party" with that room's id. Nil for the other kinds.
    • reason — why, in the words the banned player is shown.
    • source — who placed it: :admin (the admin pages), :api (the admin API), :room (a lobby host, group admin or party leader, in their own room), :plugin (a game hook) or :system.
    • banned_by / lifted_by — the person who placed or lifted it, when one did.
