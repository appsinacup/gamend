    Gamend.Bans (gamend_core v1.1.1362)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Bans: an IP address, an account or a chat sender kept out for a while, or for good.

    The bans table is the source of truth and keeps every ban after it ends, so a target's history is its rows. Gamend.Bans.Cache holds the active ones in ETS for the per-request check (banned?/2), and carries each change to the other instances over PubSub (Gamend.Bans.Sync applies them there and loads the table at boot).

    Placing a ban on a target that already has one ends the old one (lifted by whoever placed the new one) and starts the new one, so a target has at most one active ban. A target is the kind, the subject and, for a chat ban, its reach (Gamend.Bans.Ban.target/4): a global mute and a mute in one lobby are two targets.

    Gamend.Bans.ban(:ip, "1.2.3.4", expires_at: Gamend.Bans.expires_after(:timer.hours(24)))
Gamend.Bans.ban(:account, user.id, expires_at: in_a_week, reason: "Cheating", by: admin.id)
Gamend.Bans.ban(:chat, user.id, scope: "lobby", scope_ref_id: lobby.id, source: :room)
Gamend.Bans.lift(:account, user.id, by: admin.id)
Gamend.Bans.banned?(:ip, "1.2.3.4")

    An IP ban's subject is the address as the web layer keys it (an IPv6 one as its /64, GamendWeb.Plugs.IpBan); an account or chat ban's is the user id.

    Chat bans (mutes)

    A chat ban silences a sender everywhere (scope: "global", friend DMs included) or in one lobby, group or party (scope and scope_ref_id). Gamend.Chat.mute_user/4 and unmute_user/3 are chat's way in, with the room rules; muted?/3 is the per-message check. Placing and lifting one tells the player {:chat_muted, ban} and {:chat_unmuted, ...}, the user channel's chat_muted and chat_unmuted.

    Account bans

    A banned account still signs in, and can read and delete itself; everything else refuses it (the web layer: GamendWeb.Plugs.AccountBan on the API, the channels, GamendWeb.UserAuth on the website). Placing and lifting one tells the account on its user topic, {:account_banned, ban} and {:account_unbanned, user_id}, which the user channel passes on as the account_banned and account_unbanned events.

    Hooks and plugins

    A plugin bans and lifts with these functions (source: :plugin), and hears of every ban through two fire-and-forget hooks: after_ban_created/1, and after_ban_lifted/1 once for every ban that ends, lifted, replaced or expired. A ban with an end schedules a job for it (Gamend.Bans.ExpiryWorker), so an expiry is told like a lift: the hook, and account_unbanned or chat_unmuted.

    Functions

    account_ban(user_id)

    The active ban on an account, or nil. Queries only when the cache holds one, so it is cheap to ask on every request.

    active(kind, subject, opts \\ [])

    The active ban on subject, or nil: for showing its end and reason. A chat ban names its reach with :scope and :scope_ref_id.

    active?(ban)

    Whether ban is in force now.

    ban(kind, subject, opts \\ [])

    Bans subject until :expires_at (a DateTime, nil for permanent).

    banned?(kind, subject)

    Whether an IP or account subject is banned now: an ETS read, no query.

    banned_user_ids()

    The ids of the accounts banned now, as a subquery: Gamend.Accounts.Search's banned facet.

    count(filters \\ %{})

    How many bans list/2 would page through.

    expire(ban)

    Tells of a ban that ran out: drops it from the cache, tells the player (account_unbanned, chat_unmuted) and runs after_ban_lifted/1. Run by Gamend.Bans.ExpiryWorker at expires_at. :noop for a ban lifted or replaced first (that already told), {:not_yet, seconds} for one still in force.

    expires_after(ttl_ms)

    The expires_at for a ban lasting ttl_ms from now; nil for :infinity.

    get(id)

    One ban, with its users, or nil.

    lift(kind, subject, opts \\ [])

    Ends the active ban on subject, if it has one. :by is the person's user id; a chat ban names its reach with :scope and :scope_ref_id. Answers the bans it ended (none, or one).

    lift_ban(ban, opts \\ [])

    Ends this ban's target's active ban: the ban itself while it is in force (a target banned again since has a newer one, which this lifts). opts as for lift/3: :by.

    list(filters \\ %{}, opts \\ [])

    Bans, ended ones too, newest first, with the banned user and the people who placed and lifted them. Filters (string or atom keys): kind (:ip, :account or :chat, or the string), user_id, subject, scope, scope_ref_id, and active (true: only the bans in force). opts are Gamend.Query.page/2's: page, page_size.

    list_active(kind \\ nil)

    Every active ban, of one kind or all, newest first.

    muted?(user_id, chat_type, chat_ref_id)

    Whether user_id may not send in this chat: muted everywhere, or in this lobby, group or party. An ETS read, run on every outgoing message.

    prune(days)

    Deletes bans that ended (lifted or expired) more than days ago; an active ban is never deleted. days of 0 keeps them all. Answers the rows deleted. Run by Gamend.Retention with bans_days.

    @spec ban(Gamend.Bans.Ban.kind(), String.t(), keyword()) ::
  {:ok, Gamend.Bans.Ban.t()} | {:error, Ecto.Changeset.t()}

    Bans subject until :expires_at (a DateTime, nil for permanent).

    Options: :expires_at (a DateTime or an ISO 8601 string), :reason (shown to the banned player), :source (:admin by default, see Gamend.Bans.Ban), :by (the person's user id), and for a chat ban :scope and :scope_ref_id. The ban applies on this instance at once and on the others within moments.

    An account ban needs an existing user that is not an admin: banning an admin would let one admin lock another (or themselves) out of the console.
