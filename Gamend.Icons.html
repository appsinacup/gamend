    Gamend.Icons (gamend_core v1.1.1369)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    The icons people pick for what is theirs: a group's (Gamend.Groups), and an account's in place of a picture (Gamend.Accounts.User's avatar_icon). Heroicon names (rocket-launch, heroicons.com's solid set), one list for every picker (choices).

    A picked icon is stored or answered as its URL, path/1 (/icons/<name>.svg): the website serves it and draws it inline (GamendWeb.Icons, which also draws a leaderboard's, a tournament's or a quest's icon_url of that form), and a client fetches it against the server's address like an uploaded image.
