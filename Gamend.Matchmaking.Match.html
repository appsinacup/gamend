    Gamend.Matchmaking.Match (gamend_core v1.1.1406)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Creates the lobby for a claimed match and notifies the players.

    Runs outside the sweep's cluster lock: Lobbies.create_lobby/1 and join_lobby/2 fire plugin hooks and broadcasts, which must never run inside a transaction. The tickets are already claimed (status matched), so no other node can pick them up meanwhile; on failure they are requeued and retried on the next tick.

    Errors return tuples instead of raising, so one bad match never aborts the rest of the sweep.

    The game answers where a match sits and whether its players are asked first (matchmaking_seat_match/1, Gamend.MatchSeating). A match that asks is not seated here at first: start/1 opens the accept check, and accept_resolved/1 seats it once everyone accepted, or puts the ones who did back in the queue. With no answer, a match with a backfill ticket is seated in that lobby (see Gamend.Matchmaking, Backfill).

    Summary

    Functions

    accept_resolved(check)

    A matchmaking accept check resolved (called by Gamend.ReadyChecks once it passed or failed). Passed: the match is seated (seat/2). Failed: the tickets of whoever accepted, or had not answered yet when someone declined, go back in the queue, keeping their place; whoever declined or let the deadline pass has their ticket cancelled. Tickets no longer held by the match (an admin cancelled one) are left alone; a check with no tickets is not matchmaking's.

    seat(tickets, seating)

    Seats a claimed match where seating says: a lobby the game named, a new lobby with the game's settings, or by default its backfill ticket's lobby when it has one (Gamend.Matchmaking.target_ticket/1), else a new hidden, locked one. Records the lobby on the tickets and broadcasts match_found.

    start(tickets)

    A claimed match, begun: the game says how (matchmaking_seat_match/1), then its accept check is opened if that asks for one ({:accepting, check_id}), else it is seated at once (seat/2). A refusal, a hook that fails or runs past its time puts the tickets back in the queue.
