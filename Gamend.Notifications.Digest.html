    Gamend.Notifications.Digest (gamend_core v1.1.1450)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Emails that wait and go out together: a group with email_every (Gamend.Notifications.Preferences) keeps its notices here, and one email lists them.

    The clock is the user's oldest waiting notice, per group: a :week digest goes a week after the first notice in it. So nothing goes out with nothing to say, and nobody needs a time zone or a send day. An hourly sweep (Gamend.Notifications.DigestSweeper) queues what is due, and the web app's job (GamendWeb.Workers.NotificationDigest) builds and sends it, deleting only the rows it listed. A notice that arrives meanwhile waits for the next one.

    A notice whose subject is already waiting replaces that one's words and keeps its place, as the site's row does (one row per title). A user holds at most max_digest_items_per_user notices (Gamend.Limits). A digest that keeps failing (an address that bounces) is given up after 24 sweeps, a day of them: its notices are deleted, not queued forever.

    Functions

    add(user_id, group, attrs)

    Keep a notice for user_id's next digest of group. A subject already waiting there takes the new words; past the user's cap the notice is left out ({:error, :too_many_items}).

    delete(items)

    Delete exactly these rows: what arrived after they were read stays.

    due(now \\ DateTime.utc_now(:second))

    The {user_id, group} pairs due at now. A group that no longer declares email_every is due at once, so a host dropping it strands nothing.

    items(user_id, group)

    What waits for user_id in group, oldest first.

    sweep(now \\ DateTime.utc_now(:second))

    Queue every digest due at now, after giving up the notices whose digest was queued 24 times and never sent. Returns how many pairs were due.
