    Gamend.Payments.Charges (gamend_core v1.1.1535)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    The money a purchase moved (Gamend.Payments.Charge), written where it moves and read back as revenue for the admin payments page.

    Written at three points, each idempotent on (provider, kind, provider_ref):

    • the sale, when Payments.fulfill_purchase/2 first completes a purchase, keyed by its provider transaction (or order) id;
    • a renewal, for each later charge of a subscription: Stripe's charge.succeeded (keyed by the charge id) and Apple's renewal notifications (by the new transaction id, at its price). Google renewals the client validates are purchases of their own, so they arrive as sales;
    • a refund, when a refund or lost dispute ends the purchase (Payments.revoke_purchase/2), removed again by a dispute the seller won (Payments.restore_purchase/2), and the unused part of a Stripe plan refunded when a player changes plan (Gamend.Payments.Upgrades). Any other partial refund ends nothing and is not recorded.

    record_refund(purchase, opts \\ [])

    Record the money a refund or lost dispute gave back. One per purchase: revoking it twice records once. Options: amount: (the purchase's by default), currency:, and ref: for a refund that does not end the purchase (the unused part of a plan changed mid-period), keyed by the provider's refund id.

    record_renewal(purchase, ref, opts)

    Record a renewal of purchase's subscription, keyed by the provider's id for that one charge. Options: amount:, currency:, occurred_at:.

    record_sale(purchase)

    Record a purchase's sale. Runs inside its fulfilment's transaction.

    revenue_summary(opts \\ [])

    Revenue for the admin payments page, per currency (amounts never add across currencies): lifetime, this_month, last_month and last_30_days totals, the last 12 calendar months oldest first, and lifetime totals by provider and by product. Each total is %{sales, renewals, refunds, gross, refunded, net}: counts, then amounts in the smallest unit.
