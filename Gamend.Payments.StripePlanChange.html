    Gamend.Payments.StripePlanChange (gamend_core v1.1.1361)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Moving a running Stripe subscription to another product of the same period: a hosted server's size, a tier of a monthly plan. Not a longer plan (monthly to yearly), which is a new checkout (Gamend.Payments.Upgrades).

    change_stripe_subscription/4 moves the subscription's one item to the new product's price (Providers.Stripe.change_subscription_price/4), prorated and invoiced at once: a dearer product is charged its difference for the rest of the period now, a cheaper one leaves a credit on the customer's balance for the next invoices. When that invoice cannot be paid, Stripe refuses and nothing changes. end_trial: true ends a trial now, so the new product is charged in full from today: a host's free period may cover one product and not another.

    The purchase is then the new product's: its product_id and provider_product_id move, and its entitlement row moves to the new product's key (Payments.entitlement_key/2, with the purchase's subject), keeping its period, so the row a renewal or a refund later finds by source_purchase_id is the one the buyer now holds. A row already under that key is a leftover of an older purchase and is deleted, unless it is live through another purchase, which refuses the change (:already_owned). Each change is listed on the purchase (metadata["plan_changes"], newest first) and counted (payments.subscription.plan_changed).

    A price changed anywhere else (Stripe's dashboard, its customer portal) reaches the purchase the same way, from customer.subscription.updated (follow/2), so Stripe and the rows cannot disagree for longer than a webhook, even when the write after a change here failed.

    Functions

    change_stripe_subscription(user, entitlement_id, target, opts \\ [])

    Move the user's subscription behind entitlement_id to target, an active Stripe price of another subscription product with the same period, the same currency and the same per_subject. Options: end_trial: true.

    follow(purchase, subscription)

    The purchase brought to the price subscription (a Stripe subscription object) now bills: unchanged when it is the purchase's own, unknown here, or of a product a plan change cannot move to. Called on every subscription write (StripeEvents.sync_stripe_subscription/3), before its period is copied.
