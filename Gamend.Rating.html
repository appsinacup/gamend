    Gamend.Rating (gamend_core v1.1.1433)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    Skill ratings, computed: Elo and Glicko-2, for two players, teams and free-for-alls, and the gap a matcher accepts as a player waits (priv/docs/40-gameplay/32-ratings.md).

    teams = [[Rating.new()], [Rating.new()]]
[[winner], [loser]] = Rating.settle(teams, [1, 2])

    Pure: nothing here reads or writes. A game stores the numbers where it likes; dump/1 and load/1 turn one into a map and back. The SDK carries a copy of this module (mix gen.sdk).

    Summary

    Types

    score()

    How a game went for a player: 1.0 a win, 0.5 a draw, 0.0 a loss.

    t()

    A player's skill: rating, how unsure it is (deviation, shrinks with games, grows when idle) and how erratic the player is (volatility).

    Functions

    band(waited_ms, opts \\ [])

    The rating gap a matcher accepts after waiting waited_ms: it starts at :base (100), grows by :per_second (20) and stops at :max (1000), so nobody waits forever for a perfect opponent.

    dump(rating)

    A rating as a map of plain values, for KV, metadata or a leaderboard record.

    elo(a, b, score, opts \\ [])

    Elo for one game: {new_a, new_b} for ratings a and b, score being how it went for a. :k is the most one game can move a rating (32).

    expected(a, b)

    The chance a beats b, between 0.0 and 1.0. Takes ratings or plain numbers.

    glicko2(player, games, opts \\ [])

    Glicko-2 for one rating period: player after games, a list of {opponent, score}. No games means an idle period, and only the deviation grows. :tau limits how fast volatility moves (0.5; 0.3 to 1.2 is usual).

    load(stored)

    The rating dump/1 wrote. Nothing stored, or a field that is not a number, is unrated.

    new(opts \\ [])

    A player's rating, unrated unless :rating, :deviation or :volatility is given.

    settle(teams, places, opts \\ [])

    One finished match of any shape: teams (a list of lists of t/0, a team of one for a duel or a free-for-all) and each team's place in places, 1 the best and equal places a draw. Answers the teams, in order, updated.
