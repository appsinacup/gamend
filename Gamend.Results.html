    Gamend.Results (gamend_core v1.1.1370)

    What happened in a play (priv/docs/40-gameplay/45-results.md).

    A result is one play by one player (Gamend.Results.Result), its events what happened in it, in order (Gamend.Results.Event). The game writes an event when something happens and nothing else: no summary kept up to date, no copy of the play's state. The lobby holds the live state; the result is the record it leaves. The same rows are the replay (the events by id), the play at a glance (stats/1: each key's count and sum) and the player's history (list/2).

    Writes are the server's. A plugin, the host app or a hook handling a move calls start/3, record/4 and finish/1; no client writes a result. An event is one insert, and finish/1 one update that only matches an open row, so a play ends once.

    Reads follow the result's visibility, KV's words (readable?/2), and every write is pushed as it happens (result_started, result_event, result_ended) to the owner's user:<id> topic and, when its lobby's members may read it, the lobby's lobby:<id>.

    A result's events, oldest first. Opts: :after, an event id (the events written after it: a reconnect's catch-up), :limit (500, at most 2,000).

    events_by_key(result_ids, keys)

    The events of many results whose key is one of keys, by result id, each list oldest first: one query, for a page of results read whole (a history list).

    finish(result_id)

    The play over: {:ok, result}, ended now. One update that only matches an open row, so a second finish answers {:error, :already_ended}.

    get(id)

    A result by id, or nil.

    kinds()

    The kinds results have been written under, for a filter.

    list(filters \\ %{}, opts \\ [])

    Results, newest first. Filters: :user_id, :kind (one, or a list), :status, :lobby_id. Opts: :page and :page_size; with no :page, the first Gamend.Query.unpaginated_limit/0.

    move_user(from_user_id, to_user_id)

    A guest's results made the account's (Gamend.Accounts.GuestMerge): their plays join the account's history instead of going with the guest.

    prune_before(cutoff, batch)

    Results started before cutoff deleted with their events, batch at a time; answers how many results went.

    readable?(r, user)

    Whether user may read result, by its visibility: public anyone, owner_only its player, lobby_members_only a player seated in its lobby now, owner_or_lobby_member either, admin_only and every other an admin. server_only no client.

    record(result, key, value \\ nil, metadata \\ %{})

    One thing that happened in a play: key what (answer, hint), value the number it carries (or nil), metadata anything else. {:ok, event}; {:error, :ended} once the play is over, {:error, :not_found}, {:error, :too_many_events} past GAMEND_LIMITS_MAX_RESULT_EVENTS.

    record_lobby(lobby_id, key, value \\ nil, metadata \\ %{})

    record/4 on every open result of lobby_id, for code that knows the lobby and not its players (a timer, a disconnect). Answers how many it wrote.

    start(user_id, kind, opts \\ [])

    A play begun by user_id, of kind (match, boat_run): {:ok, result}. Opts: :lobby_id the lobby it runs in, :visibility who may read it (KV's words, atom or string; owner_only by default), :metadata what the play is (its settings; never its outcome).

    stats(ids)

    Each event key's count and sum of value, for one result (%{"answer" => %{count: 10, sum: 7.0}}) or a list of result ids (by id: one query whatever their number). A key whose events carry no value sums to 0.
