    Gamend.Sent (gamend_core v1.1.1531)

    Copy Markdown View Source

    What went out: one row per email (Gamend.Accounts.UserNotifier) and per push to a device (Gamend.Push.DeliveryWorker), for the admin's Sent card on /admin/notifications.

    A row keeps what the person got, so the admin can see it in its own look: an email's Gamend.Mail.Message (message/1 rebuilds it, and Gamend.Mail.Message.to_html/1 draws it in today's email layout), a push's title and text. What would let someone else act as the reader is hidden before it is stored: a sign-in or confirmation link, a code, an unsubscribe link.

    An announcement's sends share its id (batch_id), so list_lines/2 shows one send to everyone as one line, and list/2 with "batch_id" lists who got it.

    Recording never stops a send: a row that cannot be written is logged and left out. Rows go after GAMEND_RETENTION_SENT_MESSAGES_DAYS (30), and with their user.

    Record an email that was sent (or failed). opts: :user_id, :group, :batch, :status (sent), :reason, and :secrets, the strings to hide wherever they appear (a link that signs in, an unsubscribe link). A code is always hidden.
