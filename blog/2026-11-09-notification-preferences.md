# Notification Preferences

Players choose what they get and where. Email can wait and arrive as one digest.

![](notifications/preferences.png)

![](notifications/digest.png)

- **Per group, per channel**: site, email, phone.
- **One-click unsubscribe** in every email.
- **Digests** by day, week or month.
