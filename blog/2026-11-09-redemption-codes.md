# Redemption Codes

Hand out a code, and players redeem it for rewards or access.

![](redeem/redeem.png)

- **Single-use, the first N players, or every player once.** Any text, in any language.
- **Generate thousands at once**, revoke one, or download them as CSV.
- **Link to one quest** with `/redeem?quest=spring_gift`.
