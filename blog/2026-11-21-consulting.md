# Consulting and Paid Work for Gamend

Need help with your Gamend server? You can now book consulting, or pay for fixes and features.

![](/images/banner.webp)

## What I can help with

- **Deployment & hosting**: Docker, fly.io or a VPS, Postgres or SQLite, domains and backups.
- **Server scripting & hooks**: your game logic on the server.
- **Scaling & performance**: clustering and load testing.
- **Client integration**: the Godot, Unity, Unreal, C++, C#, Rust, JavaScript and Balaur SDKs.
- **Priority fixes and feature work**: a bug that blocks you fixed first, or a feature built for you.

## Pricing

- **Indies and individuals:** €50 per hour.
- **Studios** (10+ employees) or contract work: €100 per hour.
